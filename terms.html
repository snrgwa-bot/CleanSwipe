2. Description of Service

Rollwipe is a photo management application that helps users organize and clean up their photo library through an intuitive swipe-based interface. The App allows you to:

  • Review photos in your device's photo library
  • Mark photos for deletion
  • Protect favorite photos from accidental deletion
  • Delete photos from your library (deleted items go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album)
  • Use Free Up Space to find your largest photos and videos, exact copies and similar photos
  • Remove Location & Metadata (Pro): save a new copy of a photo without location and camera details
  • Edit a photo's capture date or location, shrink Live Photos and videos, add photos to albums, and hide photos in the Private Vault (Pro)
  • Subscribe to Rollwipe Pro, an optional auto-renewable subscription

Important: When you confirm deletion, photos move to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album, where they can be recovered for up to 30 days. After that, or if you empty Recently Deleted, they are gone for good. Always review your trash carefully before deleting.
